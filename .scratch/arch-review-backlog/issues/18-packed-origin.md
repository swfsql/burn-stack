## Agent brief

**Category:** enhancement

**Summary:** `Block::block_forward_packed` takes an origin: the cache that each segment
starts from. The packed forward of each container takes an optional origin for each
slot.

**Why:** burn-mamba ticket 19 moves the learnable initial state of Mamba-2 and Mamba-3
out of the block. A caller then gives a start state as a cache. The cache of a call
gives the start of each row, and the origin gives the start of each segment. The family
API of burn-mamba already takes both. A container cannot take an origin today: each
segment starts from the zero cache of its slot. So a caller can give a start state to
the rows of a stack, but not to its segments. This ticket closes that gap.

**Current behaviour:**

- `Block::block_forward_packed(&self, x, cache, options, reset)` takes no origin. At
  each reset, the block starts again from a zero cache. The doc of the method and the
  contract `packed_segments_equal_each_segment_alone` say so.
- `Layer::forward_packed`, `Layers::forward_packed` and `VocabNetwork::forward_packed`
  take the caches of the rows only. Each segment starts from the zero cache of its slot.
- `RefBlock::block_forward_packed` sets its state to zeros at each reset.
- The `Block` adapters of burn-mamba call the family `forward_packed` with
  `origin: None`.

**Desired behaviour:**

- At a reset in row `b`, the block starts again from row `b` of the origin.
- With the zero cache as the origin, each value is the same as today.
- A container takes an optional `Vec` of origins, one for each virtual layer, in the
  order of its caches. `None` gives each slot its zero cache, as for the caches.
- The positions before the first reset of a row continue from the cache of that row,
  as today.
- An origin gets a gradient from each segment that starts from it.
- Under a gradient horizon, the origin of an untracked slot makes the same backend hop
  as the cache of that slot. It gets no gradient.
- The call returns no origin. The returned caches are those of the last segment of each
  row, as today.

**Key interfaces:**

- **`Block::block_forward_packed(&self, x, cache: Self::Cache, origin: Self::Cache, options, reset)`**:
  - The origin is a cache, not an `Option`. A block always gets a cache (ticket 12), and
    a container gives the zero cache when the caller gives no origin.
  - The doc: at a reset in row `b`, the block starts again from row `b` of `origin`. The
    output of each segment is that of the segment run alone from its row of `origin`.
  - The default method still panics: a block that does not override it takes no packed
    rows.
- **`Layer::forward_packed`**: takes `origin: M::Cache` after `cache`.
- **`Layers::forward_packed`** and **`VocabNetwork::forward_packed`**: take
  `origins: Option<Vec<M::Cache>>` after `caches`. They assert one origin for each
  virtual layer, as for the caches.
- **`RefBlock::block_forward_packed`**: at a reset in row `b`, the state becomes row `b`
  of the state of the origin.
- **The contract `packed_segments_equal_each_segment_alone`**:
  - The origin is a warm cache: the cache of a forward over random tokens. Use another
    warm cache than the incoming cache, so that a block that mixes the two fails.
  - Each segment equals the segment alone from its row of the origin.
  - The positions before the first reset of a row continue from the incoming cache, as
    today.
- **The broken block of the contract tests** (`BrokenBlock`): one more breach. The block
  ignores the origin and starts each segment from the zero cache. The packed contract
  must fail on it.
- **The docs**:
  - `GLOSSARY.md` gets the term **Origin** in its packing section, after **Reset**:
    "The cache that each segment of a packed call starts from. A container gives the
    zero cache when the caller gives none." Add `_Avoid_: initial cache`. This entry
    moves here from the glossary of burn-mamba. Burn-mamba ticket 19 removes its copy.
  - The doc of each `forward_packed` above and the module header of `utils::packing`
    name the origin in place of "a zero cache".
  - The module header of `reference` says that `block_forward_packed` unrolls the
    recurrence "from a zero state at each reset". It names the origin.

**References:**

- The semantics to copy: the module header of `../burn-mamba/src/packing.rs`, and the
  doc of `Mamba2::forward_packed` in `../burn-mamba/src/mamba2/mamba2.rs`. A family

  origin.
- The hop: `BackendHop::layer` in `src/modules/layers.rs` takes the cache of one slot,
  moves it down for an untracked layer, and moves the advanced cache back up. The

- The tests to extend or copy:

  - `src/reference/tests/grad_horizon.rs`: a stack with a gradient horizon on

  - `src/block_contract/tests.rs`: `BrokenBlock` and its `Breach` cases.
- `docs/agents/testing.md`: the seams and the coverage gate of this repo.

**Constraints:**

- Read `docs/burn/autodiff-semantics.md` before you write the hop of the origins and
  the gradient tests: §1, §3 and §11. ADR-0003 gives the hop.
  - The origin of an untracked slot goes down with `cache_to_inner`, as its cache does.
    On a plain origin, this does nothing (rule 3). The origin does not come back up,
    because the call does not return it.
  - An origin from the caller follows the rules of a cache. It has the checkpointing
    strategy of the stack, or an op panics (rule 2, trap 2). An origin that an earlier
    backward used needs a detach or a round trip first (trap 11). The docs of
    `Layers::forward_packed` say this.
  - `test_device()` gives a plain device. The gradient tests run on
    `test_device().autodiff()`, as the tests of `grad_horizon.rs` do.
  - A leaf origin is `t.detach().require_grad()` on that device (trap 10). A test reads
    the gradient of a leaf with `grad`. The leaf of an untracked slot gets no gradient.
- With the zero cache as the origin, no value changes. Do not change an expected value
  or a tolerance.
- The `Cargo.toml` version goes to 0.46.0 in the first commit that breaks the public
  API. The commit message states the breaks.
- Do not change burn-mamba. Its ticket 19 changes its adapters, and it needs the pushed
  commits of this ticket. The burn-mamba build uses its pin, so it does not break in the
  meantime.
- burn-deltanet implements `Block`, so it breaks at its next move of the pin. It is
  outside the access allowlist. State the break in the commit message, and do no work
  in burn-deltanet.
- The prose check of `scripts/check.sh` compares the hard violations of each changed
  file with `origin/main`. Split each moved sentence that is over the cap.

Fixed:

- The doc of `Block::block_forward_packed` had 7 sentences on two topics. The gradient
  of the origin and the default of a container are a paragraph of their own.
- The docs and the horizon test said "untracked slot" and "tracked slot". They use the

  `the_origin_of_an_untracked_layer_gets_no_gradient`.
- The doc of `to_layer` had two phrasal verbs ("takes it down", "come back up"). It says
  "moves the cache to the inner backend" and "does not move back to the autodiff
  backend".
- "Slot" had two senses in the docs of `Layers::forward_packed` and
  `VocabNetwork::forward_packed`: a cache of the stack and an opening slot. A cache of
  the stack is "each virtual layer". The origin of a layer has one wording, "row `b` of
  its origin", in the docs of the block, the layer, the stack, the network and
  `utils::packing`.
- "The call does not return the origins." was in the paragraph on the class latents. It
  is in the paragraph on the origins.
- The header of `src/reference/tests/packed.rs` names the origins.
- `BackendHop::tracks(i)` replaces two copies of `is_none_or(|t| t[i])`.
- The test helper `rows_of` repeated `cache_row`. It is gone.
- Three names: the per-layer `packed` of the loop (another type than the `packed` of
  the call) is `packed_origin`. The `x_bsd` of the closure in the contract is
  `tokens_bsd`. The closure `zero` of the layer test is `zero_rows`.
- Spec note 1: no stack test checked "The positions before the first reset of a row
  continue from the cache of that row". In
  `a_packed_stack_starts_each_segment_from_the_origins`, the first segment of row 1
  starts at 6, and the incoming caches are warm caches that differ from the origins. The
  test compares positions 0..6 of row 1 with the stack alone from its incoming caches.
- Spec note 2: the doc of `VocabNetwork::forward_packed` said "from `origins`". It says
  "row `b` of its origin in `origins`".

Not fixed:
