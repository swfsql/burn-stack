# 12: The cache stack is one type of burn-stack

Status: resolved

Blocked by: None

From the architecture review of 2026-11-16. Line numbers are those of burn-stack
commit `b198fa5` and burn-mamba commit `7807d31b`. The review compiled nothing.

**Files:** `src/modules/cache.rs:44-66` (`CacheStack`), `src/modules/mod.rs:104-110`
(`Block::Caches`), `src/modules/layers.rs:424-450,597-629,799-808`,
`src/modules/bidi.rs:316-385`, `src/modules/hnet/cache.rs`,
`src/block_contract.rs:140,545`, `src/reference.rs:47-69`,
`../burn-mamba/src/family/adapters.rs`, `../burn-mamba/src/family/cache_traversal.rs`,
`../burn-mamba/src/family/block.rs` (`MambaCaches`), the `cache.rs` of each family.

**Problem:** Each family writes a struct around a `Vec`, a `CacheStack` impl and a
cache traversal that only forwards to the `Vec`. There are 7 such types: `RefCaches`,
`Mamba1Caches`, `Mamba2Caches`, `Mamba3DoubleSsdCaches`, `Mamba3SingleSsdCaches`,
`Mamba3Caches` and `MambaCaches`. The 5 `CacheStack` impls do not agree:

- 4 of them unwrap an empty slot in `from_slots`. `MambaCaches` keeps it.
- `Mamba3Caches::from_slots` guesses the pathway from slot 0.

The contract `cache_stack_moves_every_slot` tests only these impls. Deletion test:
with the 7 types deleted, no complexity reappears, because `modules/cache.rs` already
gives `Vec<C>` a cache traversal.

**Direction:** The containers hold `Vec<M::Cache>`. `Block::Caches` and `CacheStack`
go. The detach of a stack is one function over the cache traversal.

**Second step, same seam:** A slot is never empty. `block_forward` and `block_step`
take `Self::Cache`, and the container calls `zero_cache`. Then the rule "the zero
cache equals no cache" leaves the interface of `Block`, with its contract
`zero_cache_equals_no_cache`. `Layers::prime` with no caches is the one path that
starts from empty slots now (`layers.rs:622-627`). Decide this step at triage.

**Cost:** A break of the public API of both crates. The LM loops and the examples name
the cache stack types (`MambaCaches`, `HNetCaches`).

**Related:** `../burn-mamba/.scratch/arch-review-backlog/issues/18-pathway-option.md`
deletes the enum `Mamba3Caches` first, which makes this ticket smaller.

Split this ticket by repo at triage.

## Agent brief

**Category:** enhancement

**Summary:** The caches of a layer stack are a `Vec` of block caches, and a block
always gets a cache. `CacheStack` and `Block::Caches` go.

**Current behaviour:**

- `Block` has the associated type `Caches: CacheStack<Cache = Self::Cache>`. Each
  family writes a struct around a `Vec`, a `CacheStack` impl and a cache traversal
  that forwards to the `Vec`.
- `CacheStack` has `slot_count`, `into_slots`, `from_slots` and the provided method
  `detach`.
- `block_forward`, `block_forward_packed` and `block_step` take
  `Option<Self::Cache>`. A `None` starts from the zero cache. The contract
  `zero_cache_equals_no_cache` states that the two starts give the same values.
- `Layer::forward`, `Layer::forward_packed` and `Layer::step` take
  `Option<M::Cache>`.
- `Layers::prime` with no caches starts each slot from no cache, and the block fills
  the slot.
- `HNetStageCache` and `HNetCaches` hold `E::Caches` and `M::Caches`. They need the
  bounds `E::Caches: CacheTensors` and `M::Caches: CacheTensors`, and two
  hand-written `Clone` impls. `HNetCaches::detach` calls `cache_detach`.
- The contract `cache_stack_moves_every_slot` tests `from_slots`, `into_slots` and the
  traversal of `Block::Caches`.

**Desired behaviour:**

- Each container gives the same outputs and the same caches as before, for each call.
- The caches of a layer stack are `Vec<M::Cache>`, one cache for each virtual layer.
  A container takes `Option<Vec<M::Cache>>` and returns `Vec<M::Cache>`.
- A block always gets a cache. For a call with no caches, a container builds the zero
  caches and gives each layer its cache.
- One function detaches each kind of cache: `cache_detach`. It takes one block cache,
  the caches of a stack, an `HNetCaches` or a tuple of caches.

**Key interfaces:**

- **`Block`**:
  - The associated type `Caches` goes. `Cache: CacheTensors` stays.
  - The three calls take the cache with no `Option`:
    `block_forward(&self, x, cache: Self::Cache, options, pad)`,
    `block_forward_packed(&self, x, cache: Self::Cache, options, reset)` and
    `block_step(&self, x, cache: Self::Cache)`. The return types do not change.
  - The trait doc and the doc of `zero_cache` state the rule: a block always gets a
    cache, and a container starts a slot from `zero_cache`. A family can accept no
    cache in its own API. The paragraph "The zero cache equals no cache" goes.
- **`CacheStack`** goes, with its four methods. No module and no prelude exports it.
- **`Layer`**: `forward`, `forward_packed` and `step` take `M::Cache`.
- **The containers** keep an `Option` for the incoming caches: `Layers`,
  `BidiLayers`, `LatentNetwork`, `VocabNetwork`, `HNet` and `HNetVocabNetwork`. The
  type in the `Option` is `Vec<M::Cache>`, or `HNetCaches`.
  - `Layers::zero_caches` returns `Vec<M::Cache>`.
  - `Layers::prime` with no caches builds the zero caches when at least one latent is
    due. When no latent is due, it returns the caches as they came, `None` included.
  - Inside `Layers`, the private slot list can stay `Vec<Option<M::Cache>>`.
- **`HNetStageCache` and `HNetCaches`** keep their fields. The `encoder`, `decoder`
  and `main` fields are `Vec<E::Cache>` or `Vec<M::Cache>`.
  - The bounds `E::Caches: CacheTensors` and `M::Caches: CacheTensors` go from each
    item. No new bound replaces them.
  - `Clone` is a derive.
  - `HNetCaches::detach` goes.
- **`cache_detach`** does not change. Its doc says that one call also detaches all
  the caches of a stack.
- **`RefBlock`**: `RefCaches` goes, with its `type Caches`.
- **The `examples` module**:
  - `LmModel` keeps its associated type `Caches`. The doc of
    `LmModel::detach_caches` names `cache_detach`.
  - The sampler of tiny-stories loses the bound `M::Caches: CacheTensors`. Its
    `Prefill` holds `Vec<M::Cache>`.
- **The block contracts**:
  - `zero_cache_equals_no_cache` and `cache_stack_moves_every_slot` go, with
    `check_slots`. The two-stack contracts compare the caches of a stack with the
    cache traversal of the `Vec`.
  - Each other contract starts from `block.zero_cache(…)` where it gave `None`.
  - `padded_rows_equal_each_row_alone` runs from a warm cache and from the zero
    cache. In both runs, an empty row gives its row of the incoming cache. The special
    case "its row of the zero cache" goes.
  - The docs of the contracts say "from the zero cache", not "from no cache".
  - The module header names no removed contract and no `Block::Caches`.
- **The contract tests**:
  - The 7 tests of the **Triage notes** go.
  - The breaches `ZeroCache`, `NoCacheStep`, `EmptyRowFromNoCache`, `StackSlots` and
    `StackTraversal` go, with `BrokenCaches`.
  - `a_broken_block_without_a_breach_keeps_every_contract` does not call the two
    removed contracts.
- **The container tests**:
  - `detach_keeps_the_device_of_each_slot` calls `cache_detach` on the caches of the
    stack. Its checks do not change.
  - `detach_keeps_the_values_and_the_device_of_the_cache` of the H-Net calls
    `cache_detach`. Its checks do not change.
  - **A new test, `the_caches_of_a_stack_move_each_slot`**, goes beside
    `detach_keeps_the_device_of_each_slot`. It is a container test (ADR-0005). It
    has these parts:
    - A stack of 2 `RefBlock` layers gives two caches: the first after one window, and
      the second after one more window.
    - `assign_in_place` of the second caches into the owned buffers of the first
      caches gives each slot of the second caches, in its place.
    - The traversal of two lists with different lengths panics with "the two caches
      differ in slot count". The test checks the panic with `catch_unwind`.
- **The docs**:
  - The phrase "cache stack" goes from each doc. The docs say "the caches of a layer
    stack".
  - The module header of `modules::cache` describes the caches of a layer stack as a
    `Vec` of block caches, with no trait.
  - The header of `src/lib.rs` gives the plug-in surface with no `CacheStack` and no
    `Caches` type.
  - `README.md`: the family example has no `MyCaches` and no `CacheStack` impl. Its
    `Block` impl takes the cache with no `Option`.
  - `CLAUDE.md`: the first paragraph and the File Map line of `cache.rs` name no
    `CacheStack`.
  - `GLOSSARY.md` does not change.

### Decision 4 after the triage of burn-mamba ticket 19
