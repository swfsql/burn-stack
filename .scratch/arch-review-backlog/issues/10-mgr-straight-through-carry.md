# 10: The straight-through carry of MGR follows the carry path of each stream

Status: resolved

Blocked by: `02-residuals-transition.md`

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

The triage of `02-residuals-transition.md` on 2026-11-16 found this bug with a probe at
`f2927ae`. Ticket 02 gives the carry rule one home, so this fix edits only that home.
`11-straight-through-carry-edge-cases.md` follows this ticket.

## Agent brief

**Category:** bug

**Summary:** Under Multi-Gate Residuals, the straight-through carry adds the carry of
the pooled token to every residual stream. Make each residual stream take its own
carry through the carry path of the scheme.

**Current behaviour:**

- At the start of an untracked span, the stack takes one carry: that of the pooled
  token. At the cut, it adds that carry to the token and to every residual stream.
- The probe ran `assert_identity_span_is_exact` (the gradient-horizon container tests)
  with more streams. The test uses a stack of 4 layers and makes the bottom 2 untracked.
  Their `out_proj` is zero and their gates are closed. The gradient of the input under
  the cut differs from the gradient with no cut:

  | `n_stream` | Relative difference | Result |
  |---:|---:|---|
  | 1 (the existing test) | below `dtype_tol(TOL)` | pass |
  | 2 | 0.4648 | fail |
  | 4 | 0.8452 | fail |

- The cause:
  - The accumulation phase does not change an existing stream. Each stream that it
    appends holds the output of an untracked layer.
  - The mixing phase moves each stream towards the output of the layer. Streams do
    not mix with each other directly.
  - So across a span with zero transforms, the input reaches only the stream that held
    it. The token gets only the aggregator share of that stream.
  - The current rule over-counts, once for each extra stream.
- The doc of `Layers::grad_horizon`, the comments of the backend hop and ADR-0006 say
  that the rule is exact at the bottom of the stack. That is true only for a layer that
  outputs its input. It is false for the identity of the test, which is also the
  identity of Standard.
- The MGR paper (`../papers/multi-gate-res/tex/main.tex`) does not discuss a
  straight-through carry. But it models the carry path of each stream on Highway
  Networks: gain `1 − βᵢ` per stream, where a plain residual has gain `I`. Its gates
  start biased to carry (`b = −3`), and its trained gates are mostly below 0.1.

**Desired behaviour:**

The carry follows the carry path of the scheme, with the transforms and the gate scores
held constant.

- Standard: no change. Its carry path has gain 1.
- MGR:
  1. At the start of a span, take one carry for each residual stream that exists
     there: the stream minus its detached copy.
  2. Each residual join inside the span updates one factor for each stream, on the
     inner backend. The factor has one value for each position and stream
     (`[…, k, 1]`).

     - The mixing phase multiplies the factor of stream `i` by the `1 − βᵢ` of that
       layer.
     - The accumulation phase appends a stream with factor 0.
  3. At the cut, each stream that existed at the start of the span gets its carry
     times its factor. A stream that the span appended gets no carry.
  4. The token gets `Σᵢ αᵢ · factorᵢ · carryᵢ`. `αᵢ` is the aggregator weight of
     stream `i` at the cut, held constant.
- So at a bottom cut, only the seed stream gets a carry, and the token gets `α₀` of it.
- The factor and the weights stay in the residual-streams value of ticket 02. The owner
  of the backend hop does not change.
- `forward`, `step` and `prime` follow the same rule.
- No value of the stack changes. Only the gradients change.
- Inside a span, a suppressed skip (`ignore_first_residual`, `ignore_last_residual`)
  keeps its current carry. Ticket 11 decides that case.

**Tests:**

- The two probes are the failing tests. Each one calls `assert_identity_span_is_exact`
  with `ResidualsConfig::MultiGate { n_stream, init_bias: 0.0, init_bias_offset: 0.0,
  per_virtual_layer: false }`, once with `n_stream: 2` and once with `n_stream: 4`.
- A new exactness test with open gates:
  - It has zero transforms in the untracked layers and the init `w_beta = w_alpha = 0`.
  - It uses a `b_beta` that leaves the gates open, for example 0, so that `β = 0.5`.
  - It runs with `n_stream` 1, 2 and 4.
  - The rule above is exact here. A gain-1 rule is not exact here.
- Test 4 of the join tests of ticket 02 (the carry of each scheme) changes to the new
  rule.

## Comments

### Implementation notes

> *This was generated by AI during implementation.*

- `ResidualStreams` has two new fields:
  - `alpha`: the aggregator weights `αᵢ` of the last pool, `[…, k, 1]`. Each MGR
    join that pools sets them, also outside a span. `None` before the first pool
    (the seed, a suppressed first skip). Then the token is the one stream.
  - `span_gain`: the gain `gᵢ` of each carry path, `[…, k, 1]`. `open_span` sets it
    to 1 on the inner backend. `close_span` sets it to `None`.
- The join holds the weights outside a span for one case: a span in which no join
  pools. That is a span of one layer with a suppressed skip. Under a suppressed last
  skip at the top, the token at the cut then gets `Σᵢ αᵢ · carryᵢ`, with the weights of
  the pool below the span. The weights cost no memory on the autodiff backend, because
  the graph saves `α` for the backward of the pool. If ticket 11 rejects a suppressed
  skip in a span, the weights can live only inside a span.
- `StraightThroughCarry` is an enum: `Token` (Standard) or `Streams` (MGR,
  `[…, k₀, d_model]`). Under MGR, the straight-through carry holds `k₀` full-width
  tensors of value zero for the span, where the rule of ticket 02 held one. So a span
  under MGR saves less memory than before. The Benches line of this ticket does not
  count this cost. A bench of the span memory under MGR is a decision of the user.
- A suppressed skip in a span keeps `span_gain`. A suppressed first skip sets
  `alpha` to `None`, because the token is the one stream `F_0` there.
- `MultiGateResidual::{mix_pool, append_pool}` return a crate-private `Pooled`:
  the token, the streams, the weights `alpha` and, in the mixing phase, the gain
  `1 − β`.
  `attn_pool` keeps its signature over a private `pool` that also gives `α`. The
  public wrappers keep their signatures. No value changes.
- Tests that pin a gradient of the old rule: only test 4 of the join tests,
  `straight_through_carry_keeps_the_values_and_routes_the_gradient`. It becomes
  `straight_through_carry_follows_the_carry_path_of_each_scheme`: 4 joins, the middle 2
  with zero transforms, with and without an untracked span. 2 different streams enter
  the span, the span appends a third and then mixes, and the gate bias of each stream
  is different. A mutation check (gain 1 at the cut) fails it by 1.35.
- `assert_identity_span_is_exact` calls `assert_zero_transform_span_is_exact` with
  `UntrackedGates::Closed`. The open-gate test calls it with `UntrackedGates::Open`.
  Its messages name the config. No expected value or tolerance changes.
- The red runs: the probes failed by 0.394 (`n_stream` 2) and 1.468 (`n_stream` 4).
  Before the mixing gain, the open-gate test failed by 1.206 (`n_stream` 1). The values
  differ from those of the triage, because the random draws differ.
- The review (`tmp/arb10/review-standards.md`, `tmp/arb10/review-spec.md`) found no
  error in the math. The fixes after it:
  - The docs use "straight-through carry", not a bare "carry" (`_Avoid_` of the
    glossary). They call `αᵢ` an aggregator weight, not a share. The long sentences
    are split. The doc of `Layers::grad_horizon` gives a summary and points to the
    module header of `residuals.rs`.
  - The field `weights` is `alpha`, because the glossary uses "weight" for a
    parameter. `ResidualStreams::standard` builds the value of Standard. `resized`
    gives the shape of each new gain or zero block. The test helper takes a
    `MiddleJoins` in place of a `bool`.
  - Ticket 11 has a comment on the choices of this ticket for its cases 1 and 2.
- Review items that stay:
  - `StraightThroughCarry` mirrors the variants of `Residuals`, so a mismatched pair
    type-checks. `close_span` matches on the carry. Only `open_span` makes a carry.
  - The three MGR fields of `ResidualStreams` are separate `Option`s. A bundle would
    not fit `into_top`, which drops the streams and keeps the other two.
  - "aggregator" has no glossary term. `docs/source-terms.md` lists it as a word of
    the MGR paper, and the header of `multi_gate.rs` uses it.
