### Choices of the sweep

- **Ticket bodies and records.** A history phrase in a record changes to the
  past state and the current state. For example, a record of container-tests
  ticket 04 says that a module header "contradicted its own checks. The fixed
  header … does not contradict them". No record loses a fact.
- **Quotes of old text.** A record that quotes old text with a hard finding
  loses the quote. A short hint points to the git history of the ticket,
  which keeps the quote. This applies to the records of eight tickets and of
  this ticket. It also applies to the three quotes that tickets 02 and 06 put
  in code spans. A quote of code stays, for example a command or the current
  help text.
- **Row IDs.** The two lists of row IDs of `third-party-terms` ticket 02 had
  28 words each. One list gives `R1–R7` and `R19–R21` as ranges. The other
  list moves to a sentence of its own.
- **Return, drop, increase.** The phrasal verbs of motion become `returns`,
  `reappears`, `drops`, `increases`, "the ticket removes" or a `lift`, the
  verb of `cache_from_inner`.
- **Remove.** The ticket on the per-layer class latents used two verbs for
  one action. It uses `remove`, the word of its title.
- **Validation.** Arch-review ticket 03 says "run a validation" and "counts
  the start as a validation". So `check` names only the check script and the
  due checks.
- **Strips.** In glossary ticket 06, a derived `valid()` "strips its
  autodiff". So the end of the autodiff of a field and the end of a text have
  two different verbs.
- **Lists.** Eight long sentences become lists. Examples are the parts of
  the stack loop (arch-review ticket 14) and the messages of glossary ticket
  04.
- **Paragraphs.** Fourteen long paragraphs split by topic: ten in the
  tickets, three in the ADRs and one in `docs/agents/testing.md`. In a
  checkbox, a paragraph after a blank line can render as code. So the long
  checkbox of `MetricsLog` (arch-review ticket 03) joins two short sentences
  in place of a split.
- **Checkboxes.** A passive on the list of `## Comments` changes to
  "`## Comments` lists". Each checkbox keeps its state.
- **ADR-0003.** The option on a bare `Tensor::from_inner` had a phrasal verb.
  It is "A bare `Tensor::from_inner` to return to autodiff". No link points
  to the text of an option.
- **`autodiff-semantics.md`.** A backward frees "the branches … that no live
  tensor needs" and "the orphaned entries … that do not look useful". In
  §9.2, the row of `no_grad()` says "`train()` does not enable them", and
  `LearningResult::model` "is in validation mode". Each rule and each trap
  stays.
- **`CLAUDE.md`.** The rule on changelog prose says "an old state next to the
  current one" in place of a quote of a banned phrase. The rule keeps its
  meaning.
- **`GLOSSARY.md`.** The definition of **Straight-through carry** is two
  sentences. The term, its meaning and its _Avoid_ entry stay.
- No term of the two glossaries changes.
